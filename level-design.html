Above ground

A walled settlement

The surface is open and easy to read. One tall tower marks the centre, and everything else is placed around it.

Below ground

A settlement inside the cave

Underground the level turns vertical. Stairs, ladders and bridges link huts on different heights.

The arena

A cage over dark water

A caged fighting platform stands on pillars in a flooded cave, reached by walkways and stairs from both sides.

The mod also has a quest and characters the player can talk to. All pictures are views from the editor.
